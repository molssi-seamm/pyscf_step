User Guide#

The PySCF step runs PySCF calculations in a SEAMM flowchart. Like the other code steps it holds sub-steps; this version has one, Spin Orbit, for the spin-orbit correction in thermochemistry. Each calculation is a task run in the seamm-pyscf environment, on the job’s machine or, with a task queue, on a cluster, like any other code.

Spin Orbit#

What it computes#

Composite thermochemistry schemes treat a molecule as free of spin-orbit coupling and subtract the experimental spin-orbit lowering of the atoms (the J-averaged value, 30.3 kJ/mol for iodine). A closed-shell molecule is not free of it: spin-orbit coupling mixes excited states into its ground state and lowers its energy. For molecules with iodine this second-order term is a few kJ/mol, and leaving it out makes their heats of formation too high by about that much (2.7-3.1 kJ/mol for HI and CH3I with a CCSD(T) chain). It is larger for the heavier elements.

The step computes it as Bross, Bacskay, Peterson and Ruscic do for the Active Thermochemical Tables [Bross2023]: two-component SCF with the spin-orbit part of the pseudopotentials, minus the same scalar calculation. For a molecule M,

\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}E_\mathrm{SO}(\mathrm{M}) = E_\mathrm{2c}(\mathrm{M}) - E_\mathrm{scalar}(\mathrm{M})\\\delta_\mathrm{SO} = -E_\mathrm{SO}(\mathrm{M}) + \sum_\mathrm{atoms} E_\mathrm{SO}(\mathrm{atom}, J\text{-average})\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

\(\delta_\mathrm{SO}\) is the correction to the atomization energy that goes with the experimental atomic J-averages; it is positive (the molecule is lowered) and is added to the atomization energy, i.e. subtracted from the heat of formation. The atomic term removes the large spin-orbit energy of the core electrons a small-core pseudopotential leaves explicit (about 100 kJ/mol for iodine’s 4s4p4d), which cancels between atoms and molecule. Bross et al. found that the SCF level fits the ATcT data best (RMS 0.79 kJ/mol over 11 molecules, against 3.6 without the term).

How#

  • Pseudopotentials. The basis sets and their energy-consistent (Stuttgart-Cologne) pseudopotentials come from the Basis Set Exchange. The libraries store only the scalar potentials; the spin-orbit part is rebuilt from their j-dependent terms and checked against the published spin-orbit parameters shipped with the step (iodine ECP28MDF and bromine ECP10MDF so far). An element with a pseudopotential but no such data is refused rather than run without spin-orbit.

  • The molecule. Scalar RHF, and two-component GHF with the spin-orbit pseudopotential, at the same geometry. A check that GHF without spin-orbit equals RHF is printed.

  • The atoms. Each element with a spin-orbit pseudopotential is computed by spherical average-of-configuration SCF, scalar and two-component alike: the scalar 2P level, and the two J levels (for p5, p1/22p3/23 is J = 3/2 and p1/21p3/24 is J = 1/2), each with its own orbitals. Single determinants are not used: they break the atom’s symmetry, relax differently with and without spin-orbit and are not even reproducible. Atoms with p1 or p5 valence shells are available (the groups of B and F); those with p2, p3 or p4 (Sn, Sb, Te, Pb, Bi, …) need a small spinor CI and are refused for now.

Parameters#

Relativistic route

SO-ECP, the spin-orbit pseudopotential route above.

Method

HF, the level Bross et al. recommend.

Basis set

The basis set for the elements it covers; its pseudopotentials must have published spin-orbit data. The default is cc-pVTZ-PP.

Basis set for other elements

For the lighter elements, all-electron and without spin-orbit; default cc-pVTZ.

Uncontract the basis sets

yes (the default) uses them fully uncontracted, as Bross and Peterson recommend.

The basis set is not the cause of the differences: HI gives 2.190, 2.189 and 2.199 kJ/mol with uncontracted cc-pVTZ-PP, aug-cc-pVTZ-PP and cc-pVQZ-PP. Our values are consistently 7-10 % below Bross et al.’s, likely from their CCSD(T)-F12b geometries and the details of DIRAC’s average-of-configuration and two-component pseudopotential implementations. The offset is reported with every result and not corrected. ORCA’s all-electron CASSCF/NEVPT2 spin-orbit route gives 2.25-2.29 kJ/mol for HI.
